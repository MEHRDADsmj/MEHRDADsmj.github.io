Notification system case study

Featured case study

Multi-channel notification subsystem — Smart X

An in-house notification platform built for volume, cost control, and providers that keep changing.

Problem

Smart X needed reliable multi-channel delivery at SaaS scale without locking every channel behind a single vendor bill. Provider APIs differ; new protocols show up when the business expands.

Approach

Designed a fully flexible provider integration layer so new channels—including protocols like SMPP—plug in without rewriting the core pipeline. Used AI as a force multiplier to speed implementation while holding the line on quality through reviews, tests, and clear architecture.

Outcome

The subsystem processes 35M+ messages/day and cut provider costs by about 50% versus the prior path. Extensible integrations dropped new-provider work from roughly a week to about a day.
